# ABOW Atlas

### _From Ancient Boats to Overwhelmed Waters_

A community-contributed open data platform connecting archaeological watercraft evidence with the present-day climate vulnerability of coastal and river-dependent communities.

---

## What is this?

ABOW Atlas is an open research platform built at the intersection of maritime archaeology, GIS, and climate change. It asks a simple question:

> _The same communities that built and used boats for thousands of years are now facing rising waters, intensifying floods, and disappearing shorelines. What can the archaeological record tell us about how people have always lived with and adapted to water?_

The platform layers two kinds of data on a single map:

- **Archaeological layer** — logboat finds, watercraft sites, and historical woodland distribution
- **Climate layer** — sea level rise projections, flood risk zones, and vulnerable community areas

Anyone can contribute. Trusted community reviewers verify submissions before they are marked as confirmed. All data is published as open data for non-commercial use.

---

## Features

- Interactive map with toggleable data layers
- Popup windows for each site with photos, dimensions, wood species, and excavation context
- Community polygons showing present-day climate threats
- Sea level rise overlay (+1m scenario)
- Historic woodland distribution layer
- Filterable by region, date, and wood species _(in development)_
- Community submission and review workflow

## Contributing data

We welcome contributions from researchers, community organisations, and members of the public.

**What you can submit:**

| Type                            | Accepted formats                    |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Archaeological site record      | Text + coordinates (lat/lng or BNG) |
| Site photographs                | JPG, PNG                            |
| GIS data                        | GeoJSON, Shapefile (.zip)           |
| Community / climate information | Text + coordinates or polygon       |

**How it works:**

1. Fill in the submission form on the site
2. Your entry appears on the map immediately, marked **Unreviewed**
3. A trusted reviewer checks the submission
4. Once approved, the Unreviewed badge is removed

No account is required to submit. Submissions are public from the moment they are made.

---

## Review process

ABOW Atlas uses a lightweight community review model:

- All submissions are visible on the map from the moment they are submitted
- Unreviewed entries carry a visible **⚠ Unreviewed** indicator
- Trusted reviewers can approve entries, removing the indicator
- If you are a researcher or heritage professional and would like to become a trusted reviewer, please get in touch

## Background

This project grew out of a background in maritime archaeology and a frustration that archaeological spatial data rarely reaches beyond academic publishing. The logboat — the simplest boat ever made, just a hollowed tree — turns out to be a powerful lens for understanding how communities have always depended on forests, rivers, and coastal waters.

Those same communities are now among the most climate-vulnerable in the world.
